# 5 C: Code, Content, Capital, Collaboration, Consistent

I am very fortunate and grateful to be living in the age of AI like today.<br/>
For a Programmer like me, there are too many ~~Temptations~~ tests:

- tweak this, tweak that
- Research this, Research that
- Test product X, Y, Z
- …

and in the end it becomes: **Not Productive**

I need something to hold on to.

**5 C: Code, Content, Capital, Collaboration, Consistent**

## Word #1: Code

I place the word `Code` as the first word:

- as a _Programmer_, this is already a reflex
- I do not mean to say:
    - that `Code` is the **Most Important**
    - or that other words are **Not Important**
- I simply try to:
    - start from something Small
    - start from something Simple
    - start from something I Can do

although of course, there is a Design / Document part, as I explain in the article [Document Driven Development](/en/Document_Driven_Development/)

## Word #2: Content

I place the word `Content` as the second word:

- I mean this in the form of Articles and/or Videos
- through content, I can express myself freely {I can write with _my own writing style_}

In the article [Writing for 'Future of Me'](/en/Writing-for-Future-of-Me/), I share this in the section:

### Learned from the Internet, now sharing back

I have learned a lot from the Internet {articles / videos created by many people}.<br/>
I think it is time for me to start _sharing_ my own writing publicly.

### Writing that is useful

- useful for myself, in the "Future"
- useful for others {hopefully}

## Word #3: Capital

I am not a `Good Programmer` {if I were, I would be working at Google, Microsoft, … TopTal}<br/>
But, I ~~very~~ quite enjoy the topic of _Software Engineering_.

I place the word `Capital` as the third word, as a _reminder_ for myself:

- all of these activities require _Capital_ {read: _Money_}
- that ignoring this, will make things harder for me

```
Code     → requires time       → time is money
Content  → requires energy     → energy needs fuel
Collaboration → requires reputation → reputation is built from work
✅ Capital is not the goal — but it is crucial for all the other C's to keep running
```

## Word #4: Collaboration

I place the word `Collaboration` as the fourth word {not in position 1, 2, or 3}:

- _Collaboration / Networking_ == very important, but ideally everything should start from _Internal Factors_ first
- solid/mature _Internal Factors_ are built through the 1st and 2nd process above
- if you jump straight to _Collaboration_, without a solid/mature _Internal Foundation_? then it is easy to "Break"

```
it is not that I don't see this word as Important — but:
❌ Zero Collaboration is far better than Toxic Collaboration
```

## Word #5: Consistent

In the end, _Consistent is the key._<br/>
All the C's above — mean nothing without the word _Consistent_.

Of course, each one has its own _Challenge_.<br/>
For _Content_, I have discussed this in the article [Multi Layer when creating Content {writing, video, ...}](/en/Multi-Layer-creating-Content__writing_video/)
